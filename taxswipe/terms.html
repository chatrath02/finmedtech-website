2. Description of Service

TaxSwipe is a mobile application designed to help UK sole traders and self-employed individuals:

  • Import transactions via PDF bank statements, CSV files, or manual entry
  • Categorise business expenses according to HMRC categories
  • Calculate Income Tax and National Insurance contributions
  • Submit quarterly Making Tax Digital (MTD) updates to HMRC
  • Submit year-end annual adjustments and the Final Declaration to HMRC
  • Track tax liabilities and submission history

Scope of the Service: TaxSwipe currently supports self-employment income only. If you also receive UK property income, TaxSwipe records it to determine whether Making Tax Digital applies to you, but does not submit property updates to HMRC. If you are required to report property income under MTD, you must do so using other means alongside TaxSwipe. Partnership income is not supported.
