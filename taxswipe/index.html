Who Is This For?

  • Freelancers and contractors
  • Gig economy workers (Uber, Deliveroo, etc.)
  • Self-employed sole traders
  • Sole traders running a small business
  • Anyone with qualifying income (self-employment plus property, combined) over £50,000 — the threshold falls to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028
  • Anyone required to use Making Tax Digital from April 2026

TaxSwipe currently supports self-employment income. If you also receive UK property income, TaxSwipe takes it into account when checking whether MTD applies to you, but property updates themselves are not yet submitted through TaxSwipe.
